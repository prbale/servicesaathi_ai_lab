## What the visiting charge covers
The visiting charge pays for the technician travelling to your home and diagnosing the problem. It covers up to 30 minutes of inspection. It does not include spare parts, materials or the repair work itself.

## How visiting charges are set
Each technician sets their own visiting charge for each service category. The charge shown next to a technician when you book is the charge for that visit. The amount is fixed at the moment you confirm the booking, so later changes by the technician do not affect your existing booking.

## Repair estimates
After diagnosis, the technician gives you a written estimate for the repair before starting any work. You may accept or decline the estimate. If you decline, you pay only the visiting charge. The final repair price can be higher or lower than the visiting charge, which is why the visiting charge is not the final price.

## Spare parts
Spare parts are charged separately at the price written on the estimate. The technician must show you the old part when a part is replaced. You can choose to buy a part yourself, but workmanship on customer-supplied parts is not covered by the service guarantee.

## Payments in this demo
This learning demo does not collect real payments. All charges are simulated and no money changes hands.

## Questions about a charge
If you think a charge was wrong, raise it through the booking's support option within 7 days of the visit. Charge questions are reviewed by the demo support team.
