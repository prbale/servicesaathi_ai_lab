## How time slots work
Technicians publish one-hour visit slots. You can book any free slot in the next 7 days. Each slot is the window in which the technician is expected to arrive and start work.

## Live availability
Which technicians and slots are free changes all the time. The app always shows live availability from the booking system. This document does not list availability, and nothing in it should be read as a promise that a slot is free.

## Confirming a booking
A booking is created only after you review the booking summary and press Confirm. You then receive a booking reference that starts with SS-. Until you confirm, nothing is reserved.

## Two customers choosing the same slot
Each slot can hold only one active booking. If two customers try to confirm the same slot at the same moment, only one booking succeeds. The other customer sees a message that the slot was just taken, along with other free slots.

## Booking statuses
A booking moves through these statuses: confirmed, en route (the technician is on the way), in progress, and completed. A booking can also be cancelled by the customer or by the technician.

## Choosing a technician
The app shows technicians who offer the service you need, serve your area and have a free slot. For each technician you see why they matched, their rating, their experience and their visiting charge.
