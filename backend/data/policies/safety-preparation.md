## Before the technician arrives
Clear the area around the problem so the technician can reach it. Keep pets in another room. Have the appliance's model details or bills ready if you have them. For a water leak, closing the nearest water valve can limit damage if it is easy and safe to do.

## Electrical danger signs
If you see sparks, smoke or melted plastic, notice a burning smell, see exposed wires, or feel an electric shock, stop using that switch or appliance and keep everyone away from it. Switch off the main supply only if you can reach it safely and your hands and the floor are dry. Do not touch damaged wiring. If there is fire or someone is hurt, call the emergency number 112 first. Then book a qualified electrician to inspect it.

## Gas smell
Gas work is not covered by this service. If you smell gas, do not operate electrical switches, avoid flames, open windows if safe, leave the area and contact your gas supplier's emergency line or 112.

## No DIY repair instructions
ServiceSaathi does not give step-by-step do-it-yourself repair instructions for electrical, gas or appliance internals. These repairs can cause injury, fire or further damage. We can help you book a qualified technician instead.

## Technician safety boundaries
A technician may decline or pause work that is unsafe, such as wiring with no earthing, work at an unsafe height without proper support, or a request to bypass a safety device. The technician will explain why and suggest a safe option.

## Children and vulnerable people
Please make sure an adult is at home for the whole visit. Technicians will not carry out a visit where only children are present.
