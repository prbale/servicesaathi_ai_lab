> DEMO POLICY — fictional company "ServiceSaathi Demo Co." for a learning project. Not legal advice and not the policy of any real provider.

## Customer cancellation
You can cancel a confirmed booking online free of charge until 2 hours before the start of the visit. Within 2 hours of the start, online cancellation closes and you must contact demo support. A cancelled booking stays in your booking history.

## Rescheduling
Version 1 of the app does not move a booking to a new time directly. To reschedule, cancel the booking while online cancellation is open and then book a new slot. The new booking gets a new booking reference.

## Cancellation by the technician
A technician may cancel a confirmed booking if they become unavailable. You are notified in the app and the slot is released. You can then book another available technician.

## Released slots
When a booking is cancelled, the time slot becomes available for other customers again. The cancelled booking and its status history are kept for records.

## Late arrival and no-show
If the technician has not arrived within 30 minutes of the slot start, you may cancel through demo support without any charge. If you are not at home when the technician arrives, the technician waits 15 minutes before marking the visit as missed.

## Refunds
Because this demo collects no real payments, there is nothing to refund.
