> DEMO POLICY — fictional company "ServiceSaathi Demo Co." for a learning project. Not legal advice and not the policy of any real provider.

## Plumbing services
Plumbing covers leaking taps and pipes, blocked sinks and drains, toilet and flush repairs, shower and mixer repairs, water tank overflow valves, low water pressure checks inside the home, and installing taps, basins and sinks.

## Electrical repair services
Electrical repairs cover switches and sockets, light fittings, ceiling and exhaust fans, MCB tripping, fault finding in household wiring, new plug points, and inverter wiring inside the home. Work on the electricity board's meter or the building's main supply is not included.

## Appliance servicing
Appliance servicing covers washing machines, refrigerators, microwave ovens, geysers and water heaters, RO water purifiers, mixer grinders, dishwashers, kitchen chimneys and ovens.

## What is not covered
We do not offer air-conditioner servicing, gas pipeline or gas stove repairs, carpentry, painting, pest control, structural or civil work, or repairs to phones, laptops and televisions. Requests for these services cannot be booked.

## Areas served
In this demo, technicians serve four Pune localities: Wakad, Hinjawadi, Baner and Aundh. Requests from other areas cannot be booked yet.

## Homes only
Services are for residential homes. Shops, offices and other commercial premises are not covered in this demo.

## Workmanship guarantee
Repair work done by a technician booked through the app carries a 30-day workmanship guarantee. If the same problem returns within 30 days because of the repair, the technician revisits without a new visiting charge. The guarantee does not cover parts supplied by the customer or new, unrelated faults.
